                                                                    OneHour – A Real-World Volunteering Platform

1. What product are you planning to build?
Answer: We are planning to build OneHour, a real-world volunteering platform that connects people who want to help with orphanages, NGOs, and local community organizations that need volunteers.
2. What problem does it solve?
Answer: Many orphanages and community organizations need help with small tasks, but they struggle to find people who are willing to volunteer.
At the same time, many people want to help others but do not know where or how to start. OneHour solves this problem by connecting volunteers with organizations that need support.
3. Who are the target users?
Answer: The target users are:
Students who want to participate in social activities.
Working professionals who want to spend their free time helping others.
People who are interested in volunteering.
Orphanages, NGOs, and local community organizations that need volunteers.

4. What is the main use case?
Answer: The main use case is to help organizations post specific volunteering tasks and allow volunteers to find and participate in those tasks. For example, an orphanage might need
someone to teach children for one hour, organize books, or help with cleaning. Volunteers can view the task, register for it, complete the work, and receive confirmation from the organization.

5. What are the core features?
Answer:
Organizations can post volunteering tasks.
Volunteers can browse available tasks.
Volunteers can apply for tasks.
Volunteer history and participation tracking.
Impact tracking to show how many hours people have contributed.

6. What assumptions are you making about the users?
Answer:
People are willing to spend at least one hour helping others.
Volunteers have access to smartphones and the internet.
Users prefer simple and easy-to-use platforms.
Organizations are willing to post their volunteering requirements online.
Volunteers want to contribute to society without expecting money.
Organizations are willing to verify completed volunteering activities.

7. What will be included in your MVP?
Answer: The MVP will include only the essential features needed to connect volunteers with organizations and complete volunteering tasks.
Create and post volunteering tasks.
Browse and view available tasks.
Apply for volunteering tasks.
Accept or reject volunteer applications.
Mark tasks as completed.
Organization confirmation of completed tasks.
Basic volunteer hours tracking.

8. What will NOT be included in the MVP?
Answer: The following advanced features will not be included in the initial version:
AI-based volunteer recommendations.
Reward points and gamification.
Online donations and fundraising.
In-app chat and video calling.
Social media integration.
Advanced analytics and reports.
Paid membership or subscription plans.
GPS-based live volunteer tracking.
Multi-language support.
